Caves

PERSISTENT VOID VOLUME

Deterministic chambers and shafts exist as non-solid cells below the terrain. They can hold gas and liquid state rather than being painted background holes.

Atmosphere

O₂ + CO₂ + VAPOR

Connected cave air diffuses; CO₂ has a downward density-bias term. Low oxygen causally reduces organism function and health.

Fluids

WATER + URINE

Cave liquids are separate masses that fall and spread through void cells. Surface water can leak underground; urine can contaminate water or seep into soil.

Weather

VAPOR → CLOUD → RAIN

Cloud water is persistent state produced by condensation, advected by wind and returned to the surface as precipitation.
