No visible cell grid.

The environment still uses discrete simulation cells internally, but the default OffscreenCanvas renderer now reconstructs caves, underground liquids and cloud mass as smooth connected fields. Crawlers no longer chase final X targets directly: goals are routed through terrain-aware persistent waypoints that read slope, water depth and unstable wet ground, with damped steering and turn-safe foot release.

Navigation

PERSISTENT ROUTES

Slope, water depth and waterlogged instability influence reachability and speed. Blocked food/water goals are abandoned rather than repeatedly rammed.

Turning

DAMPED + FOOT-SAFE

Steering accumulates over time. A direction change commits only after enough steering builds, then old planted-foot contacts are released before the new gait begins.

Motion display

INTERPOLATED PRESENTATION

Rendered body, knees, feet and feelers interpolate authoritative physics positions to hide small solver chatter without changing the simulation.
