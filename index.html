Navigation

BODY-COMPATIBLE ROUTING

Surface crawlers plan over slope, water depth and unstable ground. Deep A* is capability-gated so an agent is not handed a route its motor cannot execute.

Motion

DAMPED STEERING

Heading commits before locomotion, acceleration ramps instead of snapping, and route reversals use hysteresis rather than instant left/right flipping.

Rendering

NO DEFAULT BLOCK GRID

Terrain and underground liquids are extracted from simulation occupancy and smoothed into continuous contours; creature display interpolates physics state to hide solver chatter.
